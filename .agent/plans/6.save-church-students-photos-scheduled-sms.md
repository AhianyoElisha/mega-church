# 6. Save Church, students, photos that render, and scheduled service SMS

🔴 **Complex** — a third service that shares the scanner with the other two,
a member classification that changes what the kiosk does and who gets texted,
a yearly rollover, a broken image pipeline, a crop step, and three new cron
jobs. Built as four parallel tracks on a shared foundation.

## What the church asked for

1. **Save Church** — the children's service (under 12), running *during*
   First or Second Service. Children are registered with less information,
   can never be marked at an adult service, and when one touches the one
   scanner during First or Second Service they are marked present at Save
   Church instead, and told so. Existing members who are really children must
   be movable into Save Church. Children get no SMS, ever.
2. **Students** — most of the congregation is at KNUST. Record programme and
   level, distinguish students from working adults, roll levels over each
   year with a "repeated, no change" option, and let broadcasts target
   students, non-students, or everyone.
3. **Photos** — uploaded photos show only their `alt`. Fix everywhere, and add
   a crop grid to both upload and camera capture.
4. **Scheduled SMS** — birthday text at 06:00; Saturday 18:00 reminder for
   Sunday's services (07:30 and 10:30); Wednesday 08:00 reminder for the
   18:00 midweek service; Sunday 14:00 thank-you to everyone marked present,
   worded for one service or for both.

## Shape

### `members.member_type` — one field, three values, absent reads `adult`

`'adult' | 'student' | 'child'`, an optional enum (the collection has rows, so
it cannot be added required; `benmp_partner` had the same constraint).
`memberDocToMember` narrows with `isMemberType` and reads an absent or
unrecognised value as `adult`: an adult is what every existing row was, and
the safe misreading — an adult gets texts and attends adult services, which is
exactly what happens today. `scripts/backfill-member-type.ts` writes `adult`
onto every row without one, so server-side `Query.equal` filters see every
row. Every writer supplies it explicitly.

It is **not** a widening of `home_service`. `home_service` never gates
attendance (PRD §2.1); `member_type === 'child'` does — that is its whole job.
Two facts, two fields. A child keeps a `home_service`, labelled *"Parents'
service"*: it says which service the child's Save Church session runs beside,
which is real information, not filler.

Setting `member_type` is **constituency-head tier**, beside `status` and
`title`: it decides whether the kiosk redirects somebody and whether the
church texts them. `programme` and `level` are ordinary details any head in
scope may correct.

### A child's registration needs a name and nothing else

`call_number` becomes **optional at the schema** (an explicit
`updateStringAttribute(required=false)` step in the setup script, because the
ensure-helper never touches `required` on an existing attribute) and
**required by the validator for `adult` and `student`**. For a `child` it is
optional and the form labels it *"Parent or guardian's number"*. There is no
separate guardian phone field: children are never texted, so the one number on
the row is only ever a contact for a person, and one field cannot disagree
with itself. `guardian_name` string(128), optional, records who that is.

On PATCH the rule needs the member's *resulting* type, so the validator takes
`{ partial, currentType }` and the route passes the stored type; a PATCH that
sends `call_number: null` to an adult is refused, one that sends it to a child
is accepted.

### Students carry `programme`, `level`, `level_year`

- `programme` string(128) nullable.
- `level` integer 100–800 nullable (KNUST levels: 100, 200 … ; 700/800 for
  postgraduate).
- `level_year` integer nullable — **the academic year in which this level was
  confirmed**. This is the rollover mechanism: a student is *due for update*
  when `level_year < academicYear(today)`. Nothing is edited on a schedule; the
  fact "somebody confirmed this year" is stored, and "due" is derived.
- `academicYear(todayISO)` in `lib/members/students.ts` (pure): the year if
  the month is ≥ `ACADEMIC_YEAR_START_MONTH` (8, August) else the year before.
  A constant, because the church may decide September.
- Three pure transitions, each stamping `level_year = academicYear(today)`:
  `promote` (level + 100, refused above 800), `repeat` (level unchanged),
  `graduate` (`member_type → adult`, programme/level/level_year → null).
- `/students` (admin writes, shepherd reads): the roll, filter *due for
  update*, inline edit of programme and level, bulk *Promote / Repeat /
  Graduate selected*, and an *Add students* picker that moves existing adults
  in with their programme and level entered inline.

All three student fields are refused by name (400) on a non-student, and
`member_type: 'student'` without a level is accepted — a level can be filled
in later, and refusing it would block the bulk move.

### Save Church is a COMPANION occurrence, never the open session

- `SERVICE_IDS.save = 'save-church'`, `service_slot: 'save'`, seeded and
  protected like the other two. Its Activate button is not offered and
  `POST /api/occurrences/activate` refuses it with 409: *"Save Church opens
  with First or Second Service and cannot be activated on its own."*
- `meeting_occurrences.parent_occurrence_id` string(64) nullable + index. An
  occurrence with it set is a **companion**.
- `activateOccurrence` on a `first`/`second` service also creates a Save Church
  occurrence with `parent_occurrence_id` = the new id. `closeOccurrence` closes
  every open companion first, computing each `present_count` the same way.
  Pause and resume leave companions alone: the kiosk stops on the parent's
  pause anyway, and a companion has no liveness of its own.
- `resolveOpenOccurrence` and the paused list **ignore companions** — the
  single-open rule is about who holds the scanner, and a companion never does.
  `ActiveSession` gains `companion: { occurrence, meeting } | null`.
- `ensureCompanion(session)` finds or creates today's companion for an open
  adult service. `resolveAndRecord` calls it only on the redirect path, so a
  service already open when this deploys still works.
- **The redirect**, in `resolveAndRecord`: if `member.member_type === 'child'`
  and the session's meeting is a `first`/`second` service, the target
  occurrence becomes the companion. `existingRecord`, the insert and
  `countRecords` all run against the target. Restricted and other meetings are
  unchanged — the roster decides, as it does today.
- `ScanResult.marked` and `already_marked` gain `meeting_name` (the meeting the
  mark landed in). The kiosk card says **"Marked present — Save Church"** with
  a distinct badge, and "already marked" names Save Church too. The
  `already_marked` matcher hint stays parent-only; it is a hint (CLAUDE.md).
- `/api/attendance/active` returns `companion`; the Services open card and the
  monitor header show *"Save Church: N"*. `expected` for an open adult service
  counts active members who are not children.
- Day report: a third `presenceFor(SERVICE_IDS.save)`; `DayStatus` gains
  `'save'`; export gains a *Save Church* sheet and `scope=save`. Children never
  appear as *absent* from an adult service — a child's row is `save` or
  `absent` from Save Church.
- `verify:appwrite` checks the third service row exists and is unrestricted.

### SMS: three automatic categories, one placeholder, and an audience

- `SMS_CATEGORIES` += `sunday_reminder`, `midweek_reminder`,
  `attendance_thanks`. Admin may send all three by hand (a re-run after a
  failed cron is a real need); nobody else may.
- **`{{services_attended}}`** — the one new placeholder, valid only in
  `attendance_thanks`, rendering *First Service*, *Second Service* or *both
  First and Second Service*. `render()` takes an optional `extras` map, and
  `unknownPlaceholders(body, allowedExtras)` refuses the token in any other
  category, so the empty case is impossible to express. One template serves
  both wordings; the seed shows how.
- Three routes under `/api/notifications/` (proxy-exempt, bearer or admin),
  each recording a `notification_runs` row (kinds `sunday-reminder`,
  `midweek-reminder`, `attendance-thanks`), each `automatic: true` so the
  dedupe index gives per-member-per-day idempotency:
  - `sunday-reminder` — Saturday 18:00: every active non-child member.
  - `midweek-reminder` — Wednesday 08:00: every active non-child member.
  - `attendance-thanks` — Sunday 14:00: `buildDayReport(today)` rows with
    status `first`, `second` or `both`, each rendered with its own
    `services_attended`.
- Vercel crons: `birthday-sms` moves to `0 6 * * *`; the three above are
  `0 18 * * 6`, `0 8 * * 3`, `0 14 * * 0` (Accra is UTC+0 all year).
  ~~⚠️ Vercel's Hobby plan allows two cron jobs~~ — checked 2026-10-01:
  Vercel allows 100 per project on every plan since 2026-01-20. Hobby still
  limits each to once per day (all five comply) with ±59 min timing.
- **Children are never texted.** `sendToMembers` drops `member_type === 'child'`
  targets and reports them as `excluded_children`; every route also filters
  before building targets so the count reaches the screen as `excluded` with
  a reason. Birthday SMS skips children; the birthday PUSH to the team still
  names them (a flyer for a child is fine).
- Manual sends take `audience: 'all' | 'students' | 'non_students'`
  (default `all`); the server narrows `eligible` and reports the drop. The
  `/sms` picker offers the same three so nobody picks a doomed name.

### Photos are served by the app, not by Storage

The browser builds a Storage URL with no credentials and the bucket grants
read to signed-in users only; every image is a 401 and the `alt` shows.

- `GET /api/photos/[fileId]` — any signed-in label (the kiosk needs it),
  streams the file through the admin client, `Cache-Control: private,
  max-age=31536000, immutable` (a new upload is a new id, so the URL is a
  content hash). `memberPhotoUrl()` returns that path; every caller is
  unchanged.
- Upload normalises with `sharp` (already a dependency, never imported):
  EXIF-rotate, cover-resize to 800×800, JPEG q85. The 5 MB cap stays on the
  input.
- `Avatar` falls back to initials on `onError` rather than showing broken alt.
- **Crop**: `components/photo-cropper.tsx` — square viewport, rule-of-thirds
  grid, drag to pan, slider/wheel to zoom, outputs an 800×800 JPEG. Both doors
  go through it: the file input and the camera's *"Use this photo"* hand the
  raw file to the cropper, and the cropper's *Save* is what calls the upload
  mutation. The camera stream is stopped before the cropper opens.

## Not in scope

- A separate Save Church kiosk. One scanner; the redirect is the design.
- Birth year. Still never collected; `member_type` is set by a person.
- A Wednesday service *occurrence*. The reminder is a text with a fixed time;
  the church has not asked to take midweek attendance through the kiosk.
- Amounts, pledges, or a per-student fee. Not asked.
- `{{programme}}` / `{{level}}` placeholders. Both are empty for most of the
  church, which is the `{{title}}` failure again.
