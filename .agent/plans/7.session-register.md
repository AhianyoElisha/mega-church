# 7. The register, readable in the app

⚠️ **Medium** — one pure module, one read route, one page, and one definition
of "expected" shared with the live monitor.

## What the church asked for

The Reports page lists every session with a headcount, and the only way to see
WHO that headcount was is to download a spreadsheet. The admin wants to open a
service and read the register in the app: who was there, who was not, in a
table, without leaving the page.

## Shape

### `lib/reports/register.ts` — pure

- `expectedBasis(meeting)` → `'roster' | 'adults' | 'children' | 'everyone'`.
  The ONE definition of who a session expects. `expectedFor()` in
  `lib/attendance/server.ts` now reads it too, so the register's "Expected"
  and the live monitor's cannot disagree about the same session.
- `buildRegister(expected, records, extra)` → one `RegisterRow` per expected
  member (present or absent) PLUS a row for anybody marked who is not on the
  expected list — inactive since, taken off the roster, or deleted. A mark is
  never dropped to make the list tidy; it is shown with `expected: false`.
- `registerSummary(rows)` → present / expected / absent / by method.

### `GET /api/occurrences/[id]/register`

Same readers as the record log: admin, usher, shepherd, treasurer. Returns the
occurrence, its meeting, the rows, the companion (Save Church) or parent
session when there is one, and the constituency names for the filter.

### `/reports/[id]`

Stat cards, three tabs (Present / Absent / Everyone), a name-or-number search
and a constituency filter, and the same Export button. Rows link to the
member. A session still open offers the live view.

The history table on `/reports` links each row here.

## Validation

- `lib/reports/__tests__/register.test.ts`: the basis for each kind of
  meeting; a child is not expected at an adult service and an adult is not
  expected at Save Church; an inactive member is not expected; the earliest
  of two marks wins; a mark for a member off the list is kept and flagged; a
  deleted member is a named placeholder; rows are alphabetical; the summary
  adds up.
- `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx vitest run`.
- Browser: open a past service from `/reports`, read the Present tab, switch
  to Absent, filter by constituency, search by member number, follow a row to
  the member, follow the Save Church link to the companion register.

## Not in scope

- A head (`leader`) reading a register in the app. Their one report stays the
  per-constituency download (PRD §2.9); widening that is its own decision.
- Editing attendance from the register. Marks are made at the kiosk or by
  manual check-in during the session, not corrected afterwards from a table.
