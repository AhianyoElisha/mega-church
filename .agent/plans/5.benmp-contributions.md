# 5. BENMP monthly contributions, and a reminder that only reaches the unpaid

🔴 **Complex** — a new collection, a new SMS category, the first send a `leader`
may make, and a grid that is both the report and the entry surface.

## What the church asked for

BENMP partners pay a monthly commitment towards the Global Healing Jesus
Campaign. Three things follow from that:

1. **A record of who paid which month**, across years, that they can come back
   to. Recording is done by treasurers, constituency heads and admins.
2. **New members appear on it automatically** — nobody maintains a second list.
3. **The reminder SMS reaches only partners who have NOT paid this month.**
   That is the whole reason the record exists: today a BENMP message would go
   to everybody, including people who paid weeks ago.

## Shape

### `benmp_contributions` — presence IS payment

One row per `(member_id, period)`, written only when somebody pays. No row
means not paid; there is no `paid: false` to write, to forget to write, or to
disagree with a row that says otherwise. This is `attendance_records` again —
a mark exists or it does not — and it is why a member registered in July has
no January row rather than six falses.

| field | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `member_id` | string(64) | required |
| `period` | string(7) | required, `2026-09`. ONE field, not year + month |
| `recorded_by` | string(128) | the email that ticked it |

`period` is a single `YYYY-MM` string because two integers encoding one instant
are two fields that can disagree, and because `YYYY-MM` sorts lexically in the
order it sorts chronologically — which `month=9` does not.

Unique index on `(member_id, period)`: recording twice is impossible at the
database, not merely guarded in a handler. Key index on `period` for "who is
outstanding this month".

### The month is computed in ACCRA

`currentPeriod()` derives from `todayInAccra()`, never from the browser and
never from a bare `Date`. A treasurer in another timezone, or a server in
Virginia, must not disagree with the congregation about which month it is —
that is a whole month of people either dunned early or missed.

### The reminder resolves its own recipients

`POST /api/sms/send` with `category: 'benmp'` does not trust the ids it is
given. It intersects them with the partners who are actually outstanding and
**skips the rest, reporting how many and why**. Skipping rather than refusing
the batch is deliberate: refusing means the other seventeen people do not get
their reminder because one person paid while the tab was open, and skipping
fails in the safe direction — nobody is dunned who should not be.

The UI additionally offers only outstanding partners, so nobody is presented
with a choice the server will discard. The server is the enforcement; the UI is
so nobody is offered a doomed one.

### A `leader` may now send, and only this

`canSendSmsCategory` has had no `leader` entry, and `CLAUDE.md` said so
plainly. `benmp` is the exception the church asked for, narrowed twice:

- to the ONE category — a leader still cannot send birthday, tithe or general;
- to partners in constituencies they head, resolved from `leaderScope()`.

## Not in scope

Amounts and pledges. The church asked for paid/not-paid, and an amount column
that nobody fills in is worse than no column. The shape below takes an amount
later without a migration: a nullable `amount` on a row whose presence already
means "paid".

Setting `benmp_partner` stays where it is — admin and constituency head. A
treasurer records payments and does not decide who is a partner, which is the
same line the rest of the codebase draws around member writes.
